Meowerese a13.07.2025

Meower's Notes

This is truly the proto-meowerese. This is the earliest hard record I could find of my meowerese, however I probably have some earlier bits somewhere in my Discord DMs that I'm too lazy to dig through further. The version number here comes from the date of the blog post, and the a comes from my friend suggesting it as a substitute to calling it v13.07.2025. So I guess this is an alpha release of meowerese 'w'

mrow = meow

mrowm = ok, agreement

mroww = sad

mrrow = happy

mrrm = chill mrowm

heres the original blog post from where it comes from
