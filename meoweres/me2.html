How to speak in Meowerese

Version 2
By Wangbros Louie (aka Meower :3)
2025

Part I: Humany Meowerese

Humany Meowerese is an extension of English that adds a couple of catsy words.

Major Vocabulary

Meow

Meow in Humany Meowerese has some extra uses as a noun (i.e. to replace swears), an adjective to put emphasis on words (i.e. this cat is so meowing cuddly), or a sentence ender (I feel sleepy, meow.).

Other Vocabulary

Meowity meow - general exclamation
Meowing meow - slightly swear-y meow
Meowing meows - strong swear
Purry - happy, satisfied
Hissy - angered, agitated
Tailwaggy/Tail-Waggy - happy or nervous

Part II: Catsy Meowerese

Catsy Meowerese is a language of meows encoded in English. There are no words, only sounds represented by words. I personally prefer to use mrow over meow when using Catsy Meowerese to differentiate it from Humany Meowerese.

Parts of a meow

A meow is composed of 3 parts: the start, the middle and the end. The various combinations and lengths of the possible starts, middles and ends make up different expressions. Generally, a longer middle than end means happy, and shorter middle than end means sad.

General meows

Mrow - neutral, slightly happy
Mrrow - happy, satisfied
Mroww - sad, unhappy, tired
Mra - surprised, questioning
Mraa - annoyed, disagreement, disgust(?)
Mraaa - silly, brushing offish
Mrrm - agreement
Mrrgh - agitated, annoyed
Mrowm - exclamation

The start (maybe scrap this part)

The start of the meow is most commonly "m", which is the default one (it does nothing).

Compound meows

Meows can be compounded for a greater effect.
